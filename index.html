Our Story

Born from a search for something real.

Honestly? This brand started in my kitchen. I had sensitive skin that reacted to almost everything on the shelf — fancy labels, impossible ingredient lists, and a whole lot of disappointment.

So I went back to basics. I read, I researched, I tested. And slowly, I started understanding what my skin actually needed — not what the marketing said it needed. That's when Lumière began.

Every formula here is something I personally use. If it didn't work for me, it never made it into the line. Simple as that. No fillers, no shortcuts — just ingredients that genuinely care for your skin.
